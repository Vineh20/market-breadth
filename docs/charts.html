    How to read this page

    Candles: each bar is one trading day. Green closed higher than it opened, red lower. The bars at the bottom are volume.

    MA 50/150/200: the 50, 150 and 200-day average prices. A stock above rising averages is in an uptrend.

    BOLL (Bollinger Bands) shows a normal price range; MACD shows momentum turning up or down; RSI above 70 is "overbought", below 30 "oversold"; KDJ is a fast stochastic momentum line.

    ▲ / ▼ markers on the last 3 months of the chart are bullish / bearish candlestick patterns found by TA-Lib; their names are listed below the chart. A single pattern is a hint, not a signal — it matters most at support or resistance and with volume.

    Drawing: pick a tool, then click on the chart to place points. Drawings are not saved when you change stock.
