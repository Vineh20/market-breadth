How to use this page (start here)

Every weekday evening after the US market closes, this page checks all S&P 500 stocks and lists the ones in a strong, healthy uptrend. It then points out the ones that are in a good spot for a swing trade right now. It updates itself; just refresh the page.

  1. Check the market banner first. When the market is weak, even good setups often fail. Trade smaller or not at all when it says CASH.
  2. Look at the top of the list. It is sorted best-first. Click the boxes above to show only breakouts, stocks near a buy point, and so on.
  3. Click a stock to see its chart, why it qualified, a suggested stop-loss and how many shares to buy for your risk.

Trend Template: Mark Minervini's 8 checks for a stock in a long-term uptrend (above its rising 50, 150 and 200-day averages, near its 52-week high, and outperforming most other stocks).

RS (Relative Strength): 1–99. How this stock's last 12 months compare with every other S&P 500 stock, weighted toward the last 3 months. 90 means it beat 90% of them.

Setups: BREAKOUT closed above its 20-day high on heavy volume today · NEAR PIVOT within 5% below its 20-day high (the buy point) · VCP each pullback over the last 3 months was smaller than the one before · TIGHT moved less than 8% over 10 days · DRY UP volume has gone quiet · POCKET PIVOT an up day on more volume than any recent down day.

This is a list of ideas to research, not advice to buy. Always check the chart and use a stop-loss.
